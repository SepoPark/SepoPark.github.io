About

I learn by rebuilding ideas from first principles.

My interests sit at the boundary of physics and geometry. I am drawn to questions where an abstract mathematical structure becomes physically measurable—and where a calculation exposes something intuition alone cannot.

This site is deliberately a work in progress. It will grow with research summaries, derivations, computational experiments, and clear records of what is known, inferred, or still uncertain.
